# Currency and payments

Croatia adopted the euro as its official currency in 2023, replacing the kuna.
Pricing pages and invoicing for the Croatian market should therefore be
denominated in EUR from day one — no separate currency integration is needed.

# Trade access

Croatia is a member state of the European Union, so the single-market rules we
already comply with for our existing EU customers apply unchanged.
